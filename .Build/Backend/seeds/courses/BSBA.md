Yes, you’re correct. Most **minor/general education subjects are shared across different courses**, since they are required by the Commission on Higher Education. Only a few may vary slightly depending on the program.

Now here are the **subjects for BS Business Administration (BSBA)**:

---

BS Business Administration (BSBA)

Major Subjects (Core Business):

* Principles of Management
* Principles of Marketing
* Financial Management
* Human Resource Management
* Operations Management
* Business Law
* Business Ethics
* Microeconomics
* Macroeconomics
* Business Statistics
* Strategic Management
* Entrepreneurial Management
* Organizational Behavior
* International Business
* Business Research
* Project Management

Common Specializations (depending on track):

* Marketing Management (Advertising, Digital Marketing, Sales Management)
* Financial Management (Investment, Banking, Risk Management)
* Human Resource Management (Recruitment, Training, Labor Relations)

---

Minor / General Education Subjects (shared with other courses):

* Mathematics in the Modern World
* Purposive Communication
* Understanding the Self
* Readings in Philippine History
* The Contemporary World
* Ethics
* Art Appreciation
* Science, Technology, and Society
* Life and Works of José Rizal
* Physical Education
* National Service Training Program (NSTP)

---

Note: The minor subjects are generally the same as BSIT and other programs, while the major subjects are what make BSBA different.
