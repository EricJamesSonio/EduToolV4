## **BS Computer Science (BSCS)**

### **Major Subjects (Core CS)**

1. **Introduction to Computing** – 1st Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* None

2. **Computer Programming 1** – 1st Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Introduction to Computing

3. **Computer Programming 2** – 1st Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Computer Programming 1

4. **Discrete Mathematics** – 1st Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* None

5. **Object-Oriented Programming** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Computer Programming 2

6. **Data Structures and Algorithms** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Object-Oriented Programming, Discrete Mathematics

7. **Algorithms and Complexity** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Data Structures and Algorithms

8. **Automata Theory** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Discrete Mathematics

9. **Programming Languages** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms

10. **Software Engineering** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Object-Oriented Programming

11. **Database Systems** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Computer Programming 2

12. **Operating Systems** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Data Structures and Algorithms, Computer Architecture

13. **Computer Networks** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Operating Systems

14. **Artificial Intelligence** – 4th Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Data Structures and Algorithms, Algorithms and Complexity

15. **Machine Learning (optional in some schools)** – 4th Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Artificial Intelligence, Data Structures and Algorithms

16. **Human-Computer Interaction** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Software Engineering

17. **Computer Architecture** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Introduction to Computing, Object-Oriented Programming

18. **Numerical Methods** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Discrete Mathematics

19. **CS Thesis / Capstone Project** – 4th Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Completion of all core CS subjects
