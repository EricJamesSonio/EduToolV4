BS Information Technology (BSIT) subjects are usually divided into major (core) and minor (general education) subjects.

Major Subjects (Core IT):

* Introduction to Computing
* Computer Programming 1
* Computer Programming 2 (Object-Oriented Programming)
* Data Structures and Algorithms
* Database Management Systems
* Web Systems and Technologies
* Software Engineering
* Human-Computer Interaction
* Operating Systems
* Computer Networks
* Information Assurance and Security
* Systems Analysis and Design
* IT Project Management
* Capstone Project / Thesis

Minor / General Education Subjects:

* Mathematics in the Modern World
* Purposive Communication
* Understanding the Self
* Readings in Philippine History
* The Contemporary World
* Ethics
* Art Appreciation
* Science, Technology, and Society
* Life and Works of José Rizal
* Physical Education
* National Service Training Program (NSTP)
