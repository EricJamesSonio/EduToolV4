## **BS Criminology**

### **Major Subjects (Core Criminology)**

1. **Introduction to Criminology** – 1st Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* None

2. **Criminal Law** – 1st Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Introduction to Criminology

3. **Criminological Theories** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Introduction to Criminology

4. **Criminalistics / Forensic Science** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Criminal Law

5. **Law Enforcement Administration** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Criminological Theories

6. **Crime Detection and Investigation** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Criminalistics / Forensic Science, Law Enforcement Administration

7. **Police Administration** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Law Enforcement Administration

8. **Correctional Administration** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Police Administration

9. **Juvenile Delinquency** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Criminological Theories

10. **Criminal Psychology** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Criminological Theories, Juvenile Delinquency

11. **Ethics and Moral Values in Law Enforcement** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* None

12. **Disaster and Risk Management** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Law Enforcement Administration

13. **Criminal Investigation Practicum / Field Training** – 4th Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Crime Detection and Investigation, Police Administration

14. **Community Policing and Public Safety** – 4th Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Criminal Investigation Practicum / Field Training

15. **Research in Criminology** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Criminological Theories, Criminal Law
