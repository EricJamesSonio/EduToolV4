## **BS Accountancy (BSA)**

### **Major Subjects (Core Accounting)**

1. **Fundamentals of Accounting** – 1st Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* None

2. **Financial Accounting and Reporting I** – 1st Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Fundamentals of Accounting

3. **Management Accounting** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Financial Accounting and Reporting I

4. **Cost Accounting** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Management Accounting

5. **Auditing Theory** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Cost Accounting, Financial Accounting and Reporting II

6. **Auditing and Assurance Services** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Auditing Theory

7. **Advanced Financial Accounting and Reporting** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Financial Accounting and Reporting I & II

8. **Taxation (Income Tax, Business Tax)** – 3rd Year, 2nd Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Financial Accounting and Reporting I

9. **Regulatory Framework and Legal Issues in Business** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
   *Prerequisite:* Business Law

10. **Business Law** – 2nd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* None

11. **Accounting Information Systems** – 2nd Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Fundamentals of Accounting

12. **Strategic Cost Management** – 4th Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Cost Accounting

13. **Financial Management** – 3rd Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Management Accounting

14. **Governance, Business Ethics, Risk Management, and Internal Control** – 4th Year, 1st Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Auditing Theory

15. **Accounting Research** – 4th Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Advanced Financial Accounting and Reporting, Management Accounting

16. **Integrated Review Courses (for board exam preparation)** – 4th Year, 2nd Sem
    *Prerequisite:* Completion of all core accounting subjects
