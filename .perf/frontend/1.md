### Important details

**1. Don't remove caching behavior just to reduce requests.**

`useAppQuery` applies the selected `QUERY_PRESETS` and then lets individual query options override those defaults. That ordering is intentional and should be preserved unless we find a specific caching problem.

**2. The landing page's videos already use intersection-based loading.**

`SolutionSection.tsx` creates the video element only after its container approaches the viewport. That is better than immediately loading every video on page entry. The remaining questions are whether the browser fetches more media than necessary and how switching between roles affects playback.

**3. The carousel's triple rendering needs careful treatment.**

The current animation relies on three copies of the organization list. Simply removing two copies could break the infinite-scroll effect. We should examine the data size and the CSS behavior before optimizing it.

**4. Avoid changing all three global fonts prematurely.**

The font families may serve different purposes, such as marketing headings, body text, and code. First, we need to confirm where each is used in the stylesheets. Removing a font without that check could change the appearance across the entire application.
